### Hi there 👋

# Who Am I?

I'm **Aurel**, a software engineer from Cotonou, Benin.

I like building the parts of a product that nobody sees: the auth, the data
layer, the thing that turns a model into a feature people actually use. And
when I want to understand how something works, I write it from scratch rather
than read about it. That is how I ended up with a ray tracer and a neural
network in C++ sitting next to production TypeScript.

- 🎓 Just wrapped up my Bachelor in Computer Science at **Epitech Benin**
- 🛠️ Spent the past year as the sole developer of an **AI tutoring product** for
  francophone West Africa, from the Figma redesign through to production
- 🧠 Deep in **LLM systems** at the moment: retrieval, embeddings, context
  management, and the unglamorous work of making them behave
- 🎮 **Godot** on the side. I built a voxel engine from scratch because I wanted
  to know how Minecraft does it
- 👥 President of **Game Cloud**, Epitech Benin's game development society, in 2025
- 🧑‍🏫 Teaching assistant at Epitech, following **100+ students** through their
  projects: code reviews, one-to-one guidance, delivery assessments. I keep at
  it outside school too, from a digital literacy week in rural Benin to a talk
  on tech careers in Kétou with 10000 Coders
- ✍️ I write fiction when I am not writing code, and I read a lot of both
